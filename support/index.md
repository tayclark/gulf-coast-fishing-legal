# Gulf Coast Fishing support

Gulf Coast Fishing shows wind, tides, waves, water temperature and fishing rules for spots off Louisiana and Mississippi, all on one map. It is published by Coast Software.

## Contact us

Email **[support@coast.software](mailto:support@coast.software)**. Tell us your phone model, the app version (shown in the App Store or Play Store listing) and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

For privacy questions, see the [privacy policy](../privacy/).

## Common questions

**Where does the data come from?**
Public government services: NOAA tide stations and buoys, the National Weather Service forecasts and marine alerts, NOAA CoastWatch satellite water temperature, and NOAA nautical charts. Size limits, bag limits and seasons come from Louisiana Wildlife and Fisheries, the Mississippi Department of Marine Resources and NOAA Fisheries. In the app, every card names its source, and the Data Sources screen lists them all.

**How fresh is it?**
Each card shows how old its reading is. The app saves what it last downloaded for up to a day, so it keeps working when you lose signal and labels anything out of date.

**Can I use it to navigate?**
No. Gulf Coast Fishing is for planning only. The chart view is not for navigation. Use official charts, your own instruments and your own judgment on the water.

**Are the fishing rules official?**
No. They are a summary to help you plan, and rules can change at short notice. Check the agency that manages the water you're fishing before you keep a fish. The regulations card links to each agency.

**Where are my spots saved?**
Only on your phone. There's no account and nothing to sync. Deleting the app deletes your spots.

**Why does the app ask for my location?**
To centre the map and sort spots by distance. Your position stays on your phone. The app works without it.
