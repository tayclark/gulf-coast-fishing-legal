# Gulf Coast Fishing privacy policy

**Last updated: October 18, 2026**

Gulf Coast Fishing ("the app") is published by Coast Software ("we"). This policy explains what the app keeps on your phone, what it sends over the internet, and who receives it.

The short version: you don't need an account, your spots and your location stay on your phone, and we don't sell, share or track anything about you.

## What stays on your phone

- **Your spots.** The spots you save, along with their names, locations, categories and species, are stored only on your device. We have no server, so there is nothing to sync, back up or read.
- **Your location.** If you allow location access, the app reads your position on the device to centre the map, sort spots by distance and show how far away they are. Your position is never sent to us or to anyone else.

## What the app sends, and to whom

The app shows conditions from public government data services. To fetch them, it sends requests directly from your phone:

| Recipient | What it receives | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NOAA National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) | The coordinates of a spot you open, plus a few spots near you that the app loads in advance | Wind, wave and marine forecasts for that spot |
| NOAA CoastWatch ERDDAP | The coordinates of a spot you open | Satellite water temperature |
| NOAA CO-OPS and NDBC | Which tide station or buoy is being looked up | Tides, currents and buoy observations |
| NOAA Office of Coast Survey | The map area on screen while the Chart layer is on | The nautical chart overlay |
| Apple Maps (iOS) or Google Maps (Android) | The map area on screen | The base map and satellite imagery |
| Sentry | Crash reports (see below) | Fixing bugs |

None of these requests include your name, an account, or your phone's own location. Like any internet request, they show your device's IP address to the service that receives them. Each service handles that under its own policy:

- NOAA: <https://www.noaa.gov/protecting-your-privacy>
- Apple: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>
- Google: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- Sentry: <https://sentry.io/privacy/>

## Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, so we can fix the problem. A report contains the error, where in the app's code it happened, the app version, and basic device details such as the model and operating system version.

Reports don't include your name, an account or your location: the app removes user and request details and any coordinates from the error reports it sends. Sentry records the IP address a report came from, as web services do; we use it only to tell problems apart, never to identify you. Reports are not linked to you and are not used for tracking or advertising. Sentry deletes them after its retention period.

## What we don't do

- No accounts and no sign-in.
- No advertising, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.
- We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't collect any to sell.

## Deleting your data

Everything personal lives on your phone. Delete the app and it's all gone. We don't hold a copy. If you want us to delete crash reports from your device, email us with roughly when the app crashed and we'll delete the matching reports we can find.

## Children

The app is for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If the app starts collecting or sending anything new (for example, a paid feature that needs a server), we'll update this page before that version is released and change the "Last updated" date above.
